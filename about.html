Meet Our Doctors

At Excel Dental of Moon Township, our mission is to combine advanced dental treatments with a personal, compassionate touch, so every patient feels at ease.

Get to know Dr. Neha Sharma and Dr. Meer Ahmed, and learn about their experience, approach to care, and the people and interests that inspire them.

General Dentist

Dr. Neha Sharma

Dr. Neha Sharma brings over 15 years of clinical experience and a passion for exceptional, patient-centered care to every smile she treats. She earned her Bachelor of Dental Surgery degree in India and went on to complete her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine.

Highly proficient in root canals, extractions, restorative dentistry, implantology, and cosmetic dentistry, Dr. Sharma combines advanced clinical skills with a gentle approach to help patients achieve healthy, confident smiles in a comfortable and welcoming environment.

Outside of the office, Dr. Sharma enjoys spending quality time with her husband and two sons. She loves cooking and gardening, finding joy in creating and nurturing—both at home and in her dental practice. Her commitment to family, precision, and personalized care helps patients feel relaxed, heard, and confident in their treatment from the moment they walk through the door.

General Dentist

Dr. Meer Ahmed

Dr. Meer Ahmed has been practicing dentistry for 15 years and is a friendly, dedicated general dentist in Moon Township, PA. He enjoys getting to know his patients and takes his passion for dental care and their smiles to heart. Dr. Ahmed believes in a personalized approach, building genuine connections with his patients and providing excellent, individualized care.

Before working as a dentist in Moon Township, Dr. Ahmed completed his education in India and pursued further education at the Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Glasgow in the UK. He worked in renowned hospitals in the Middle East, where he honed his clinical skills. To advance his career, Dr. Ahmed immigrated to the U.S. to study at the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine. He completed his master's and doctoral degrees with many awards and honors.

Dr. Ahmed stays current with developments in dentistry through continuing education and active membership in several organizations, including the American Dental Association (ADA), Pennsylvania Dental Association (PDA), and Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Glasgow (RCPSG). His areas of expertise include surgical extractions, root canals, crown and bridge treatments, implants, Invisalign, and removable dentures.

Dr. Ahmed believes he can make a difference in patients' lives by involving them in treatment decisions, educating them on how to maintain a healthy smile, and helping them achieve not only a healthy mouth but a healthy body.

When not working, Dr. Ahmed loves coming up with new recipes in the kitchen, traveling with his family, and cuddling with his kids.
